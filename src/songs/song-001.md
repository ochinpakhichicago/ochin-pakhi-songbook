[Glossary]
khejur gachh | খেজুর গাছ | date-palm tree — source of the sweet winter sap (ros) tapped by climbers; the song's central image
hari | হাঁড়ি | a small earthen clay pot tied to the tree trunk to collect the dripping sap overnight
ros | রস | the sweet sap collected from the date-palm — also carries the folk double-meaning of "essence" or the sweetness of life/love
gora poche | গোড়া পচে | the base/root rots — what happens if the sap is left to drip away uncollected instead of being carefully tended
okale hobe mowron | অকালে হবে মরণ | an untimely death — the tree (or the neglected heart) perishes before its time
roshi / dori | রশি / দড়ি | the rope used by the tapper to climb, secure the pot, and steady himself on the tree
jhulan | ঝুলন | hanging, swinging — the pot suspended high on the trunk to catch the falling sap
jowar (joubon) | যৌবন | youth, prime — here, the tree's peak season of sap production
kaushal | কৌশল | skill, technique — the careful method required to tap the tree without harming it
tole pora | তলে পড়া | falling down below — the real danger faced by the tapper high on the tree, echoed as a warning to the heart
gachhi | গাছি | a professional date-palm sap-tapper, a traditional rural Bengali occupation, especially in winter
surosik | সুরসিক | one who relishes and savors the essence (rosh) — a connoisseur of sweetness
ros ashwadon | রস আস্বাদন | tasting and savoring the sap
