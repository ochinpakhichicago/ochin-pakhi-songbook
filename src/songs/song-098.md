[Refrain]
T: Kichhudin mone mone ghorer kone
B: কিছুদিন মনে মনে ঘরের কোণে
E: For some days, quietly, in the corner of your home

T: Shyamer pirit rakh gopone
B: শ্যামের পিরিত রাখ গোপনে
E: keep your love for Shyam hidden

T: Isharay koibi kotha gothe mathe
B: ইশারায় কইবি কথা গোঠে মাঠে
E: Speak only in gestures, out in the pasture and fields

T: Dekhish jeno keu na jane
B: দেখিস যেন কেউ না জানে
E: See to it that no one comes to know

T: Keu na bojhe keu na shone
B: কেউ না বোঝে কেউ না শোনে
E: Let no one understand, let no one hear

[Verse 1]
T: (Boli) Shyamke jokhon porbe mone
B: (বলি) শ্যামকে যখন পড়বে মনে
E: (I say) when you find yourself thinking of Shyam,

T: Chaibi kalo megher pane
B: চাইবি কালো মেঘের পানে
E: look toward the dark clouds instead

T: Ar rannashale kandbi bose
B: আর রান্নাশালে কাঁদবি বসে
E: and sit and weep in the kitchen,

T: Bhije kath diye unune
B: ভিজে কাঠ দিয়ে উনুনে
E: feeding damp wood into the stove — let the smoke take the blame for your tears

[Verse 2]
T: (Boli) Shyam-sayore naite jabi
B: (বলি) শ্যাম-সায়রে নাইতে যাবি
E: (I say) go and bathe in the Shyam-lake

T: Jolke porosh korbi kene
B: জলকে পরশ করবি কেনে
E: why not let the water touch you

T: Sayore shatar diye asbi phire
B: সায়রে সাঁতার দিয়ে আসবি ফিরে
E: swim across the lake and come back

T: Boli gayer boson bhijbe kene
B: বলি গায়ের বসন ভিজবে কেনে
E: I say, why shouldn't your clothes get soaked through

[Verse 3]
T: (Boli) Utor jabi sotor hobi
B: (বলি) উতর যাবি সতর হবি
E: (I say) go north, and stay veiled/hidden

T: Bolbi ami jai dokkhine
B: বলবি আমি যাই দক্ষিণে
E: but say that you are going south

T: Ar moner kotha mone thake
B: আর মনের কথা মনে থাকে
E: and let the matter of your heart remain in your heart alone

T: Oroshike janbe kene
B: অরসিকে জানবে কেনে
E: why should the unfeeling ever come to know it

[Glossary]
Shyam | শ্যাম | Krishna's dark-hued epithet ("the dark one") — the beloved addressed throughout Baul and Vaishnav love poetry
pirit | পিরিত | love, romantic devotion — a folk form of প্রীতি/প্রেম
gothe mathe | গোঠে মাঠে | in the cattle-pasture and open fields — the everyday rural spaces where a secret glance or gesture could pass unnoticed
kalo megher pane chaoya | কালো মেঘের পানে চাওয়া | gazing toward the dark monsoon clouds — Krishna's complexion is traditionally likened to storm clouds, so looking skyward becomes a disguised way of looking at him
rannashale kandbi bose, bhije kath diye unune | রান্নাশালে কাঁদবি বসে, ভিজে কাঠ দিয়ে উনুনে | weep while sitting at the hearth, feeding it damp wood — the smoke, not sorrow, will be blamed for your tears
Shyam-sayor | শ্যাম-সায়র | the "Shyam-lake" — a poetic pond-name fusing the beloved's name with the bathing place, giving an alibi for wet eyes and wet clothes
porosh | পরশ | touch
kene | কেনে | dialectal/rhetorical "why" — functions here as an implied imperative ("why not," i.e. "you should"), not a literal question
utor / sotor | উতর / সতর | dialectal Baul-Bengali, likely উত্তর (north) and a term for veiling/concealment — the couplet plays on giving a false travel direction as a cover story
oroshik | অরসিক | one without "rosh" (aesthetic-emotional sensibility) — a person incapable of appreciating or understanding deep love, from whom the secret must be kept

[Arrangement]
A classic Baul song of concealed devotion by Nabani Das Khaypa Baul (father of Purna Das Baul, a foundational figure of the modern Bengal Baul tradition). Text cross-checked against a published lyrics source; the "utor/sotor" line carries some dialectal ambiguity even in that source.
