[Glossary]
bnaadh bhenge dao | বাঁধ ভেঙে দাও | break the dam, the barrier — a call to shatter constraints and outworn structures
bondi praanomon | বন্দী প্রাণমন | the imprisoned life and mind — a soul held captive by convention or fear
shukno gaang | শুকনো গাঙ | a dry riverbed — a channel emptied of vitality, awaiting revival
bonyar uddam koutuk | বন্যার উদ্দাম কৌতুক | the flood's wild, unruly play — life's overflowing vital force
bhangonero joyogaan | ভাঙনেরও জয়গান | the victory-song of breaking — celebrating destruction as the necessary prelude to renewal
jirno puraton | জীর্ণ পুরাতন | the decayed, the old — outmoded structures destined to be swept away
maabhoi | মাভৈ | Sanskrit for "fear not" — a classical exhortation against fear, used often by Tagore
ajana | অজানা | the unknown
durdaro bege dhaao | দুর্দম বেগে ধাও | rush forward with untamable, unstoppable force
