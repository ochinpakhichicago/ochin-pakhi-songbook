[Refrain]
T: Graamchhaara oi ranga maatir poth
B: গ্রাম ছাড়া ঐ রাঙা মাটির পথ
E: That red-earth road, leading out of the village,

T: Aamar mon bhulay re.
B: আমার মন ভুলায় রে।
E: steals away my heart

[Verse 1]
T: Ore kaar paane mon haat baariye
B: ওরে কার পানে মন হাত বাড়িয়ে
E: Oh, toward whom does my heart stretch out its hands,

T: Lutiye jaay dhulay re.
B: লুটিয়ে যায় ধুলায় রে।
E: and fall, rolling, into the dust?

[Verse 2]
T: O je aamay ghorer baahir kore,
B: ও যে আমায় ঘরের বাহির করে,
E: It is that very road that draws me out of my home,

T: Paaye paaye paaye dhore
B: পায়ে পায়ে পায়ে ধরে
E: catching hold of me, step by step by step —

T: Mori hay hay re
B: মরি হায় হায় রে
E: ah, alas, alas

[Verse 3]
T: O je kere aamay niye jaay re
B: ও যে কেড়ে আমায় নিয়ে যায় রে
E: It snatches me away and carries me off,

T: Jaay re kon chulay re.
B: যায় রে কোন চুলায় রে।
E: off — to who knows what furnace, what fate?

T: O kon baaNke ki dhan dekhabe,
B: ও কোন বাঁকে কি ধন দেখাবে,
E: At which bend will it show me what treasure,

T: Konkhane ki daay thyakabe -
B: কোনখানে কি দায় ঠেকাবে -
E: at which place will it land me in what trouble —

T: Kothay giye shesh mele je
B: কোথায় গিয়ে শেষ মেলে যে
E: where it finally comes to an end,

T: Bhebei na kulay re.
B: ভেবেই না কুলায় রে।
E: my thoughts simply cannot fathom

[Glossary]
ranga matir poth | রাঙা মাটির পথ | a red-earth road — the reddish laterite soil roads of rural Bengal (especially the Birbhum/Santiniketan region), evoking rustic wanderlust
mon bhulano | মন ভুলানো | bewitching, enchanting the mind — leading the heart astray with fascination
haat bariye dhulay lutano | হাত বাড়িয়ে ধুলায় লুটানো | stretching out a hand and falling, rolling into the dust — a gesture of helpless yearning
ghorer bahir kora | ঘরের বাহির করা | drawing someone out of the house — the road's pull away from settled domestic life
paaye paaye dhora | পায়ে পায়ে ধরা | catching hold step by step — being gradually, inexorably drawn along
kon chulay jaoa | কোন চুলায় যাওয়া | going to who-knows-what furnace — an idiom for an unknown, possibly perilous destination
baaNk | বাঁক | a bend or turn in the road
daay thyakano | দায় ঠেকানো | running into trouble or difficulty
bhebe na kulano | ভেবে না কুলানো | unable to fathom something no matter how much one thinks

[Arrangement]
Dotara riff with light flute (bansuri?) improvisation. After the accordion solo, stay sparse and low-volume until "Kon baaNke," then build to a hot tempo and hold it until "Konkhane." Tabla drops out before the last chorus on the tihai, then nate and sam land one by one, then dotara while voices go round four times, ending together.
