[Glossary]
bonde | বন্দে | a Sylheti dialect form of "bondhu" (friend/beloved) — addressing the beloved, whether earthly or divine
maya lagano | মায়া লাগানো | casting a spell of attachment — maya (illusion/enchantment) taking hold of the heart
pirit / piriti | পিরিত / পিরীতি | love, from প্রীতি
iter bhata | ইটের ভাটা | a brick kiln — a fierce, industrial image invoked for the intensity of love's burning
dewana | দেওয়ানা | a madman, one crazed by love — from Persian "diwana"
bichchheder onol | বিচ্ছেদের অনল | the fire of separation — biroho, the pain of longing for an absent beloved
kaliza angar | কলিজা আঙ্গার | the heart/liver turned to charcoal — a vivid folk idiom for a heart burned raw by suffering
kulnasha piriter nesha | কুলনাশা পিরীতের নেশা | an intoxicating love that destroys family honor — "kul" (lineage/honor) + "nasha" (intoxication/ruin)
kul o man | কুল ও মান | family honor and reputation, dignity — sacrificed to the intoxication of love
baul | বাউল | the wandering mystic-singer tradition; here also Shah Abdul Karim's own bhanita signature within the song
