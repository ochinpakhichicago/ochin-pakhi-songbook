[Glossary]
ghor bari bhala na | ঘর-বাড়ি ভালা না | my house and home are not good/secure — a statement of impermanence, that no earthly house can be made truly lasting
shunnyero majhar | শূন্যেরও মাঝার | in the midst of the void — a metaphysical space, suggesting existence itself is built upon emptiness
ayna | আয়না | mirror
pakna chul | পাকনা চুল | grey, whitened hair — a sign of approaching old age and death
ghor duaar na bandha | ঘর-দুয়ার না বান্ধা | refusing to build house and door — declining to invest in impermanent worldly shelter
Hason Raja | হাসন রাজা | the poet's own bhanita signature — Hason Raja was a 19th-century mystic poet and zamindar of Sylhet
dalan kutha | দালান কুঠা | a grand pucca/brick mansion — a symbol of worldly wealth and permanence
rongin kora | রঙিন করা | painting or decorating brightly in color
