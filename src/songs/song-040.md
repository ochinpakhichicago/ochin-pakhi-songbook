[Refrain]
T: Eso nipobone chhayabithitole, eso karo snan nabodharajole
B: এসো নীপবনে ছায়াবীথিতলে, এসো করো স্নান নবধারাজলে
E: Come into the nipa-grove, beneath the shaded avenue of trees, come and bathe in the fresh stream of new rain

T: Dao akulia ghano kalo kesh, paro deho gheri meghonilo bes
B: দাও আকুলিয়া ঘন কালো কেশ, পরো দেহ ঘেরি মেঘনীল বেশ
E: Let your thick, dark hair fall loose and free, wrap your body in a cloak of cloud-blue,

T: Kajolonayone, juthimala gole, eso nipobone chhayabithitole
B: কাজলনয়নে, যূথীমালা গলে, এসো নীপবনে ছায়াবীথিতলে
E: with kohl-lined eyes, a garland of jasmine at your throat — come into the nipa-grove, beneath the shaded avenue of trees

[Verse 1]
T: Aaji khone khone hasikhani, sokhi
B: আজি ক্ষণে ক্ষণে হাসিখানি, সখী
E: Today, moment by moment, let that smile, O friend,

T: adhorenayone uthuk chamoki
B: অধরনয়নে উঠুক চমকি
E: flash suddenly upon your lips and eyes

T: Aaji khone khone
B: আজি ক্ষণে ক্ষণে
E: today, moment by moment

[Verse 2]
T: Mollarogane tabo madhusware dik bani aani banomormore
B: মল্লাররাগে তব মধুস্বরে দিকবাণী আনি বনমর্মরে
E: In the raga Mallar, with your sweet voice, carry word to every direction through the forest's rustling murmur,

T: Ghanoborishone jalokalokale eso nipobone chhayabithitole
B: ঘনবরিষণে জলকলকলে এসো নীপবনে ছায়াবীথিতলে
E: amid the dense downpour and the babbling of water — come into the nipa-grove, beneath the shaded avenue of trees

[Glossary]
nipobon | নীপবন | a grove of nipa/kadamba trees — trees strongly associated with the monsoon and with Krishna in Bengali poetry
chhayabithi | ছায়াবীথি | a shaded avenue or pathway of trees
nabadhara jol | নবধারাজল | the fresh new stream of rainwater — the first rains of monsoon
meghonilo bes | মেঘনীল বেশ | a garment the color of cloud-blue, dark rain-clouds
kajolonayon | কাজলনয়ন | kohl-lined eyes
juthimala | যূথীমালা | a garland of jasmine flowers
mollarogo (Raga Mallar) | মল্লাররাগ | Raga Mallar, the classical raga traditionally associated with rain and the monsoon season
bonomormor | বনমর্মর | the rustling murmur of the forest
jalokalokal | জলকলকল | the babbling, gurgling sound of flowing water
