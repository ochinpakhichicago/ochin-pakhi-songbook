[Glossary]
bhadro, ashwin | ভাদ্র, আশ্বিন | the Bengali calendar months corresponding roughly to mid-August through mid-October, the turn from monsoon to autumn
baaper ghor | বাপের ঘর | the father's house — a married woman's natal home, as opposed to her husband's home
bodhu | বধূ | a young bride or wife, a term of tender address
holud bata | হলুদ বাটা | ground turmeric paste, used ceremonially before a wedding — here "gone stale," marking the passage of time since the marriage
pirit golar phnashi | পিরিত গলার ফাঁসি | love become a noose around the neck — longing turned into a binding, almost suffocating ache
porob din | পরব দিন | a festival day
dhamsha, madol | ঢামসা, মাদল | traditional folk percussion instruments, especially associated with tribal festival music
shimul phul | শিমুল ফুল | the silk-cotton tree's blossom, which blooms a vivid scarlet red
ghungur | ঘুঙুর | ankle bells worn while dancing
mohua phul | মহুয়া ফুল | the mahua tree's flower, known for its sweetness and traditional use in a fermented drink
phultushi | ফুলতুসী | a small nectar-feeding bird, likely a sunbird or flowerpecker
