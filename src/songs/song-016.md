[Refrain]
T: Madhuro Madhuro dhoni baje
B: মধুর মধুর ধ্বনি বাজে
E: A sweet, sweet sound resounds

T: Hridoyokamolo bonomajhhe
B: হৃদয়কমল-বনমাঝে
E: within the lotus-grove of my heart

[Verse 1]
T: Nibhritobasini binapani amritomurotimoti bani
B: নিভৃতবাসিনী বীণাপাণি অমৃতমুরতিমতী বাণী
E: She who dwells in seclusion, veena in hand, her voice itself a form of nectar

T: Hiranokirano chhobikhani paranero kowtha se biraje
B: হিরণকিরণ ছবিখানি — পরানের কোথা সে বিরাজে
E: A golden-rayed image — where within my heart does she reside?

[Verse 2]
T: Modhu ritu jage dibanishi pikokuhorito dishi dishi
B: মধুঋতু জাগে দিবানিশি পিককুহরিত দিশি দিশি
E: Spring stays awake day and night, the cuckoo's call filling every direction

T: Manosomadhupo padotale murochhi porichhe porimale
B: মানসমধুপ পদতলে মুরছি পড়িছে পরিমলে
E: The bee of my mind falls fainting at her feet, lost in fragrance

T: Eso debi, eso ee aloke, ekbar tore heri chokhhe
B: এসো দেবী, এসো এ আলোকে, একবার তোরে হেরি চোখে
E: Come, O goddess, come into this light, let me behold you with my own eyes, just once

T: Gopone thekona monoloke chhayamoyo mayamoyo saje
B: গোপনে থেকো না মনোলোকে ছায়াময় মায়াময় সাজে
E: Do not stay hidden in the realm of the mind, veiled in shadow and illusion

[Glossary]
madhuro | মধুর | sweet — the opening word sets the song's tone, a sound so sweet it stirs the heart
hridoyokamolo | হৃদয়কমল | the lotus of the heart — the heart imagined as a lotus grove where the sound reverberates
nibhritobasini | নিভৃতবাসিনী | she who dwells in seclusion — the goddess/muse residing in a private, hidden inner space
binapani | বীণাপাণি | "veena in hand" — a classical epithet for Saraswati, goddess of music, learning and speech
amritomurotimoti bani | অমৃতমুরতিমতী বাণী | a voice that is itself the embodiment of nectar — speech so sweet it is immortal ambrosia given form
hiranokirano | হিরণকিরণ | golden-rayed — describing the divine, luminous image of the goddess
paraner kotha | পরানের কোথা | where, within the heart/soul — "paran" (soul/heart) + "kotha" (where), the poet asking where in his innermost self she resides
modhu ritu | মধুঋতু | spring, the sweet season — traditionally the season of renewal and longing in Bengali poetry
pikokuhorito | পিককুহরিত | filled with the cuckoo's koohoo-call — pik (cuckoo) + kuhorito (calling out), a classic image of spring longing
manosomadhupo | মানসমধুপ | the bee of the mind — the poet's own mind/thoughts imagined as a bee drawn helplessly to her fragrance
murochhi porichhe | মুরছি পড়িছে | falls fainting, swoons — overwhelmed to the point of collapse
porimale | পরিমলে | in fragrance — the scent that intoxicates the mind-bee
debi | দেবী | goddess — the divine feminine presence being invoked
monoloke | মনোলোকে | in the realm/world of the mind — the inner, mental plane where she remains concealed
chhayamoyo mayamoyo saje | ছায়াময় মায়াময় সাজে | dressed in shadow and illusion — the poet pleads for her to step out of veiled, elusive form into visible presence
