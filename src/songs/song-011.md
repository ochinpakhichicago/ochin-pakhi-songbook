[Refrain]
T: Jete jete ekla pothe
B: যেতে যেতে একলা পথে
E: Going along the lonely path

T: Nibechhe mor baati.
B: নিবেছে মোর বাতি।
E: my lamp has gone out

T: Jhar esechhe, ore, ebar
B: ঝড় এসেছে, ওরে, এবার
E: A storm has come, oh, this time

T: Jharke pelem saathi.
B: ঝড়কে পেলেম সাথি।
E: I have found the storm as my companion

T: Aakaashkone sarboneshe
B: আকাশ-কোণে সর্বনেশে
E: In the corner of the sky, all-destroying,

T: Kshane kshane uthechhe hese,
B: ক্ষণে ক্ষণে উঠছে হেসে,
E: it bursts into laughter again and again

T: Praloy aamar keshe beshe
B: প্রলয় আমার কেশে বেশে
E: Destruction, in my hair and my attire,

T: Korchhe maatamaati.
B: করছে মাতামাতি।
E: is running riot

[Verse 1]
T: Je path diye jetechhilem
B: যে পথ দিয়ে যেতেছিলাম
E: The path along which I was travelling

T: Bhuliye dilo taare,
B: ভুলিয়ে দিলো তারে,
E: has itself been made to vanish from memory

T: Aabar kotha chalte habe
B: আবার কোথা চলতে হবে
E: Where must I go now, once more,

T: Gabheer andhakaare.
B: গভীর অন্ধকারে।
E: into this deep darkness

T: Bujhi ba ei bajrarabe
B: বুঝি বা এই বজ্ররবে
E: Perhaps in this thunder's roar

T: Nutan pather baarta kabe -
B: নূতন পথের বার্তা কবে -
E: the message of a new path — when will it come —

T: Kon pureete giye tabe
B: কোন পুরীতে গিয়ে তবে
E: in which realm, once I arrive there,

T: Prabhaat hobe raati.
B: প্রভাত হবে রাতি।
E: will the night turn into dawn

[Glossary]
ekla pothe | একলা পথে | alone on the path — the solitary traveller's journey with no companion in sight
baati | বাতি | lamp — the light guiding the traveller, now extinguished
jhar | ঝড় | storm — arrives as an unexpected companion once the lamp is gone
sarboneshe | সর্বনেশে | all-destroying, calamitous — describes the ominous laughter rising in the sky
praloy | প্রলয় | cataclysm, apocalypse — cosmic destruction/dissolution, here personified as wild and unrestrained
keshe beshe | কেশে বেশে | in hair and attire — a dishevelled, wind-whipped appearance, destruction shown through disorder in dress and hair
maatamaati | মাতামাতি | frenzy, riotous abandon — chaotic, unrestrained revelry
bhuliye dilo | ভুলিয়ে দিলো | made to forget — the storm has erased the known path from memory
gobhir andhokar | গভীর অন্ধকার | deep darkness — the dense night the traveller must now walk into
bajrarab | বজ্ররব | the roar of thunder — read here as a possible herald or message
nutan pather barta | নূতন পথের বার্তা | the message/tidings of a new path — the hope that the storm signals a different way forward
pureete | পুরীতে | in the realm, abode, or city — the unknown destination the traveller wonders about
prabhat | প্রভাত | dawn — the turning of night into morning, the hoped-for resolution

[Arrangement]
Raga: Mallar | Taal: Jhampak | Composed: 12 September 1914, Surul (Puja Parjay). Notation by Dinendranath Tagore.
