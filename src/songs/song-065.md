[Glossary]
Gaji Pir | গাজী পীর | a Muslim folk saint venerated in the Sundarbans as protector of forest-goers, woodcutters, and animals — especially against tiger attacks
Bonbibi | বনবিবি | the "Lady of the Forest," a syncretic Hindu-Muslim forest goddess worshipped by Sundarbans communities for protection from tigers and other dangers
jari | জারি | a traditional Bengali narrative folk-song genre, often devotional or historical, especially within Muslim Bengali folk culture
badabon | বাদাবন | the mangrove forest — another name for the Sundarbans
Bongojononi | বঙ্গজননী | "Mother Bengal" — the land personified as a protective mother
bain, goran, golpata, hetal | বাইন, গরান, গোলপাতা, হেঁতাল | specific mangrove tree/plant species of the Sundarbans, traditionally used for local house-building
keora | কেওড়া | a mangrove fruit tree, its sour fruit used in local cooking
bhetki, bhangan, topshe, parshe, bele | ভেটকি, ভাঙন, টপসে, পারশে, বেলে | local fish species caught in the Sundarbans waterways
Jewel Aich, P.C. Sarkar | জুয়েল আইচ, পি.সি. সরকার | celebrated Bengali stage magicians, invoked metaphorically for the "magic" of Sundarbans honey's taste
mowal, baowal | মৌয়াল, বাওয়াল | a traditional honey-collector, and a traditional woodcutter, of the Sundarbans
Aila, Sidr | আইলা, সিদর | two devastating cyclones that struck the Sundarbans region, in 2009 and 2007 respectively
Nilkontho | নীলকণ্ঠ | Shiva's epithet as "blue-throated one," for swallowing poison to save creation — applied here to the Sundarbans absorbing environmental harm to protect the wider world
unnoyoner jhor | উন্নয়নের ঝড় | "the storm of development" — a critical metaphor for unchecked industrial/developmental encroachment threatening the forest
mabhoi | মাভৈ | Sanskrit for "fear not," used here as a rallying refrain
chingri machher bora | চিংড়ি মাছের বড়া | fried prawn fritters, a beloved regional snack

[Arrangement]
A contemporary jari-style folk song about the Sundarbans mangrove forest, its guardian figures, local livelihoods, wildlife, and the environmental threats it faces, by Musa Kalim Mukul of Satkhira.
