[Glossary]
gun tana | গুণ টানা | hauling the tow-rope — the backbreaking labor of a junior boatman who walks the riverbank pulling the boat forward by rope; the lowest-ranked task on a boat crew
pachhay bosha | পাছায় বসা | to sit at the stern — the mark of a senior boatman or helmsman, who steers rather than hauls; moving from the rope to the stern is a mark of advancement

[Discussion.Summary]
A boatman's joyful announcement to his family: Allah has promoted him from the hardest labor on the boat — hauling the tow-rope along the riverbank — to the privileged seat at the stern. He asks his uncle to carry the news to his aunt, who stood by him through his hard times ("the days when fortune was against me"). A classic Bhatiyali theme: the river as the measure of a life's fortune, and gratitude voiced through family rather than directly to God.
