[Refrain]
T: Mon chhara ki moner manush boy
B: মন ছাড়া কি মনের মানুষ বয়
E: Without the mind, can the Man of the Heart ever appear?

T: Je jemon banchha kore tar kachhe se uday hoy
B: যে যেমন বাঞ্ছা করে তার কাছে সে উদয় হয়
E: As one desires, so does He rise up before them

[Verse 1]
T: Moner manush monohara
B: মনের মানুষ মনোহরা
E: The Man of the Heart is captivating beyond measure

T: Rosmoy rup rose bhora
B: রসময় রূপ রসে ভরা
E: His form itself is made of rasa, brimming with sweetness

T: Dekhle habi attohara
B: দেখলে হবি আত্মহরা
E: If you behold Him, you will lose yourself entirely

T: Odharake dhora day
B: অধরাকে ধরা দায়
E: yet to grasp the ungraspable is no easy task

[Verse 2]
T: Bishuddho manusher karon
B: বিশুদ্ধ মানুষের করণ
E: The way of the pure Man is this —

T: Chondi rojokinir moron
B: চণ্ডী রজকিনীর মরণ
E: like the death of Chandi and Rajakini

T: Ek marone dujon moron
B: এক মরণে দুজন মরণ
E: in a single death, two die together

T: Attay attay mishe roy
B: আত্মায় আত্মায় মিশে রয়
E: soul merges into soul, remaining as one

[Verse 3]
T: Gosai Matan Chade bhone
B: গোসাঁই মাতান চাঁদে ভনে
E: Gosai Matan Chand declares,

T: Manush dhora manush bine
B: মানুষ ধরা মানুষ বিনে
E: catching the Man is impossible without a Man to guide you

T: Ami pabo ki ar ei jibone
B: আমি পাব কি আর এই জীবনে
E: Will I ever attain, in this very life,

T: Sei manush sei rosmoy
B: সেই মানুষ সেই রসময়
E: that Man, that being of pure rasa?

[Glossary]
moner manush | মনের মানুষ | the "Man of the Heart" — the central Baul concept of the true inner being sought within through sadhana
banchha | বাঞ্ছা | desire, wish
uday hoy | উদয় হয় | rises up, appears, manifests
monohara | মনোহরা | captivating, heart-stealing
rosmoy rup | রসময় রূপ | a form made of rasa — divine aesthetic essence, sweetness, ecstasy
attohara | আত্মহরা | self-forgetting — overwhelmed to the point of losing one's own ego
odharake dhora | অধরাকে ধরা | grasping the ungraspable — the central Baul paradox that the formless divine can only be "caught" through embodied devotion
bishuddho manush | বিশুদ্ধ মানুষ | the "pure Man" — the perfected inner being
Chondi Rojokini | চণ্ডী রজকিনী | Chandi and Rajakini — referencing the legendary devotion of poet Chandidas for the washerwoman Rajakini, a Baul/Sahajiya symbol of transcendent, boundary-breaking union
ek marone dujon moron | এক মরণে দুজন মরণ | in a single death, two die — the mystical merging of two selves into one, ego-death as spiritual union
gosai | গোসাঁই | an honorific for a Vaishnav saint or spiritual teacher
bhone | ভনে | the bhanita — the poet's own signature declaration within the song
manush dhora manush bine | মানুষ ধরা মানুষ বিনে | catching the true Man is impossible without a realized Man to guide you — the Baul teaching that a living guru is essential to spiritual attainment
