[Glossary]
anjali | অঞ্জলি | an offering made with cupped hands, especially in worship — here, the poet offers his song itself as an anjali
prodeepo shikha | প্রদীপ-শিখা | a lamp's flame — the trembling flame as a metaphor for the poet's quivering, devoted heart
debalaya | দেবালয় | a temple, a sacred dwelling place
aaroti nritto | আরতি-নৃত্য | the arati-dance — ritual movement performed during aarti, waving light before a deity
shawtodal | শতদল | the "hundred-petaled lotus" — an image of the heart or of love blossoming fully
rupe rawshe tolomolo | রূপে-রসে টলমল | trembling, swaying unsteadily in form and essence — overwhelmed with emotion
podotole ronjite | পদতলে রঞ্জিতে | coloring the ground at the feet — an act of humble devotion, offering words like flowers strewn at the beloved's feet
