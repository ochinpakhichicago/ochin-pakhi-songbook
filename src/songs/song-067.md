[Refrain]
T: Kon name dakile tare
B: কোন নামে ডাকিলে তারে
E: By whatever name you call Him,

T: Hrid-akashe udoy hobe
B: হৃদাকাশে উদয় হবে
E: He will rise in the sky of your heart

T: Apnar apni fana hole
B: আপনার আপনি ফানা হলে
E: When your self dissolves into your own self,

T: Se bhed jana jabe
B: সে ভেদ জানা যাবে
E: then that mystery will be known

[Verse 1]
T: Arbi te bole Allah
B: আরবীতে বলে আল্লাহ
E: In Arabic they call Him Allah

T: Farsi te koy Khoda tala
B: ফার্সীতে কয় খোদাতালা
E: in Persian they say Khoda-Tala

T: God bolichhe Jissu r chyala
B: গড বলিছে যিশুর চ্যালা
E: Jesus's disciples call Him God

T: Bhinno deshe bhinno bhabe
B: ভিন্ন দেশে ভিন্ন ভাবে
E: different in different lands, in different ways

[Verse 2]
T: Allah horir bhajan pujon
B: আল্লাহ হরির ভজন পূজন
E: The worship of Allah and of Hari alike

T: Ei sokol Manusher srijon
B: এই সকল মানুষের সৃজন
E: all of this is Man's own creation

T: Achanok ochenay jokhon
B: অচানক অচেনায় যখন
E: When, suddenly, in the unknown,

T: Gyanindriyo na sombhobe
B: জ্ঞানেন্দ্রিয় না সম্ভবে
E: the senses of knowledge cannot reach at all

[Verse 3]
T: Moner bhab prokashite
B: মনের ভাব প্রকাশিতে
E: To express the feelings of the mind,

T: Bhashar srishti e jogote
B: ভাষার সৃষ্টি এ জগতে
E: language was created in this world

T: Onamok odhor ke chinte
B: অনামক অধরকে চিনতে
E: but to recognize the nameless, ungraspable One,

T: Bhasha bakkyo nahi pabe
B: ভাষা বাক্য নাহি পাবে
E: language and words will never be enough

[Verse 4]
T: Apnar apni hole fana
B: আপনার আপনি হলে ফানা
E: When your self dissolves into your own self,

T: Dekha deben Sai Rabbana
B: দেখা দেবেন সাঁই রব্বানা
E: the Divine Lord will reveal Himself to you

T: Siraj Sai koy Lalon kana
B: সিরাজ সাঁই কয় লালন কানা
E: Siraj Sai says, blind Lalon tells,

T: Swarupe rup dekh songkhepe
B: স্বরূপে রূপ দেখ সংক্ষেপে
E: see your true form within, in essence, in brief

[Glossary]
fana | ফানা | self-annihilation, the dissolution of the ego into the divine — a Sufi-derived term central to Lalon's philosophy
hrid-akash | হৃদাকাশ | the "sky of the heart" — the inner space within which the divine reveals itself
Allah, Khoda, God, Hari | আল্লাহ, খোদা, গড, হরি | the different names various traditions give to the same ultimate reality — central to Lalon's name-transcending, syncretic philosophy
gyanindriyo | জ্ঞানেন্দ্রিয় | the sense-organs of knowledge — the ordinary faculties through which worldly knowledge is gained, which fail before the divine
moner bhab | মনের ভাব | the inner feeling or state of the mind
onamok odhor | অনামক অধর | the nameless, ungraspable — the divine, beyond all naming and form
Sai Rabbana | সাঁই রব্বানা | the Divine Lord — "Sai," a Baul/Fakir term of address for God or guru, joined with "Rabbana" (Arabic, "our Lord")
Siraj Sai | সিরাজ সাঁই | Lalon's own spiritual guru, frequently invoked in his bhanita
Lalon kana | লালন কানা | "Lalon the blind" — Lalon Fakir's self-referential signature within his songs
swarup | স্বরূপ | one's true, essential form or nature, as opposed to outward appearance
