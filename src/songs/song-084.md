[Glossary]
nutan jouboneri dut | নূতন যৌবনেরই দূত | the messenger/herald of new youth — Tagore's epithet for the vibrant, rebellious spirit of youthful energy
chanchal, adbhut | চঞ্চল, অদ্ভুত | restless/fickle, and strange/extraordinary — qualities worn here as a badge of pride
bera bhanga | বেড়া ভাঙা | breaking down fences — a metaphor for youthful rebellion against constraint
ashokboner raanga neshay raangi | অশোকবনের রাঙা নেশায় রাঙি | coloring oneself in the crimson intoxication of the ashoka grove — dyed in the vivid, passionate hue of spring blossoms
jhanjharo bandhono | ঝঞ্ঝার বন্ধন | the bonds of the storm — paradoxically, even storms are imagined as having restraints that youth tears apart
bidyut | বিদ্যুৎ | lightning — a symbol of sudden, unstoppable, electrifying force
agaadh jol | অগাধ জল | fathomless water — deep, perilous depths
jujhiye kul paoa | জুঝিয়ে কূল পাওয়া | struggling and fighting one's way to the shore — overcoming hardship through sheer effort
jiban maron jhar | জীবন মরণ ঝড় | the storm of life and death — a moment of ultimate crisis

[Arrangement]
From the play "Taser Desh" (The Land of Cards) — the Prince's song in Act 1. Gitabitan #588. Composed 1340 Bengali year (1933–34 CE). Bichitra Parjay.
