[Refrain]
T: Bidhir bnadhon kaatbe tumi emon shoktimaan -
B: বিধির বাঁধন কাটবে তুমি এমন শক্তিমান -
E: You will cut through fate's own bindings, so mighty are you —

T: Tumi ki emni shoktimaan !
B: তুমি কি এমনি শক্তিমান!
E: are you truly that mighty?

[Verse 1]
T: Aamader bhaanga gara tomar haate
B: আমাদের ভাঙা গড়া তোমার হাতে
E: Our making and unmaking lies in your hands —

T: emon obhimaan -
B: এমন অভিমান -
E: such is your pride —

T: Tomader emni obhimaan.
B: তোমাদের এমনি অভিমান।
E: such is your very pride

[Verse 2]
T: Chirodin taanbe pichhe, chirodin raakhbe niche -
B: চিরদিন টানবে পিছে, চিরদিন রাখবে নিচে -
E: You cannot forever hold us back, forever keep us down —

T: Atoi bawl naai re tomar, saibe na sei taan.
B: এতই বল নাই রে তোমার, সইবে না সেই টান।
E: you do not have that much strength; that pull will not endure

[Verse 3]
T: Shaasone jatoi ghero
B: শাসনে যতই ঘেরো
E: However tightly you encircle us with your rule,

T: aachhe bawl durbalero,
B: আছে বল দুর্বলেরও,
E: even the weak have their own strength,

T: Hawo na jatoi baro aachhen bhagoban.
B: হও না যতই বড়ো আছেন ভগবান।
E: however great you may become, God still stands above you

[Verse 4]
T: Aamader shokti mere torao bnaachbi ne re,
B: আমাদের শক্তি মেরে তোরাও বাঁচবি নে রে,
E: Killing off our strength, you too will not survive,

T: Bojha tor bhaari holei dubbe torikhan.
B: বোঝা তোর ভারি হলেই ডুববে তরীখান।
E: once your burden grows too heavy, your very boat will sink

[Glossary]
bidhir bnadhon | বিধির বাঁধন | fate's bindings, decree — constraints imposed as if ordained by destiny
shoktimaan | শক্তিমান | mighty, powerful
bhaanga gara | ভাঙা গড়া | making and breaking — creation and destruction, here meaning one's fate entirely in another's hands
obhimaan | অভিমান | a pride mixed with reproachful self-regard — here, the oppressor's arrogant self-image
shaasone ghera | শাসনে ঘেরা | encircling with rule, imposing authority and restriction
durbalero bawl | দুর্বলেরও বল | the strength even the weak possess — an assertion that oppression cannot fully erase resistance
shokti mere bnaacha | শক্তি মেরে বাঁচা | surviving after killing off another's strength — a rhetorical challenge, arguing the oppressor's own survival depends on the oppressed
bojha bhaari hole tori dube | বোঝা ভারি হলে তরী ডোবে | if the burden grows too heavy, the boat sinks — a warning that unchecked oppression brings its own downfall

[Arrangement]
Swadesh Parjay. Composed 1905–1911, written in response to the Partition of Bengal.
