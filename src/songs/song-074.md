[Glossary]
ghaat | ঘাট | a landing or dock on a riverbank
dingaa | ডিঙা | a small boat, a dinghy
pan | পান | betel leaf, chewed as a gesture of hospitality, love, or intimacy in Bengali culture
bNashi (bashi) | বাঁশি | the flute — addressed directly here as if a person, standing in for Krishna the flute-player, a classic motif in Vaishnav and Baul poetry
Allah'r dohai | আল্লাহ্‌র দোহাই | "in Allah's name," "I beg you by God" — an oath-like refrain
poran | পরান | life, soul — "poraner binimoy" means "in exchange for one's very life"
baner taan | বনের তান | melodies of the forest — associated with Krishna's flute-playing in the woods
ronge ranga hoya | রঙে রাঙা হওয়া | becoming dyed in someone's color — a metaphor for union, becoming immersed in the beloved's essence
piri | পিঁড়ি | a small, low wooden stool traditionally offered to a guest
teen er pakhani | তিনের পাখানি | the "boat/vessel of the Three" — an esoteric Baul/mystic image; its precise doctrinal meaning is uncertain
tin rosh | তিন রস | the "three essences" — likely a symbolic triad in Baul spiritual practice; exact meaning uncertain
gunomoni | গুণমণি | "jewel of virtue" — an epithet of praise for the beloved
